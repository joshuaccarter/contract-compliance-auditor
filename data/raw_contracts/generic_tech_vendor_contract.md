# Master Cloud Services Agreement (MSA)
**Vendor:** Apex Cloud Technologies, LLC ("Vendor")  
**Client:** Enterprise Purchasing Corp ("Customer")  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026  

## Section 3: Fees, Invoicing, and Payment
3.1 Subscription Fees. Customer shall pay all subscription fees specified in applicable Order Forms. 
3.2 Invoicing Schedule. All undisputed fees shall be invoiced annually in advance. Invoices are payable within thirty (30) calendar days from the date of the invoice. 
3.3 Fee Adjustments. Upon the conclusion of the Initial Term or any Renewal Term, Vendor reserves the right to increase annual subscription fees by up to seven percent (7.0%) upon forty-five (45) days prior written notice.

## Section 5: Term, Renewals, and Termination
5.1 Term and Auto-Renewal. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of twelve (12) months ("Initial Term"). This Agreement shall automatically renew for additional successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal at least fifteen (15) days prior to the expiration of the current Term.
5.2 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days of receiving written notification.
5.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Customer's access to the SaaS platform terminates immediately. Customer shall have fourteen (14) days to self-export Customer Data using native tools, after which Vendor may permanently delete all stored records.

## Section 8: Service Availability & Warranties
8.1 Uptime Commitment. Vendor will use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure the SaaS Services maintain an operational System Availability of at least ninety-nine and nine-tenths percent (99.9%) during each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.

## Section 11: Limitation of Liability
11.1 Consequential Damages Waiver. TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.
11.2 Liability Ceiling. EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY UNDER SECTION 9, VENDOR'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE LIMITED STRICTLY TO THE FEES ACTUALLY PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

## Section 14: General Provisions
14.1 Governing Law. This Agreement and any dispute arising hereunder shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Both parties consent to personal jurisdiction within Delaware courts.
