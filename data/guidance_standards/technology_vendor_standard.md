# Enterprise Vendor Contracting Guidance & Compliance Standard
**Document Ref:** STD-PROC-2026-V1  
**Target Scope:** Cloud Software, SaaS, and Technology Services  

Every inbound technology agreement must be evaluated against the following 10 mandatory baselines:

| Item # | Focus Area | Requirement Baseline | Acceptable Fallback / Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **REQ-01** | Service Level Agreement (SLA) | Monthly uptime guarantee of at least 99.9%. | 99.5% with financial credits. |
| **REQ-02** | SLA Service Credits | Minimum 10% monthly service fee credit for each 0.5% outage below threshold. | Escalating fee credit schedule. |
| **REQ-03** | Auto-Renewal Notice Window | Written notice of non-renewal required no earlier than 60 days prior to expiration. | 45–60 days window. Under 30 days is non-compliant. |
| **REQ-04** | Termination for Convenience | Customer may terminate for convenience with 30 days written notice. | 60 days written notice without penalty. |
| **REQ-05** | Termination Assistance / Transition | Vendor must provide 60 days transition support and export client data at no added cost. | 30 days support; standard T&M rates. |
| **REQ-06** | Payment Terms | Payment due Net 60 days from invoice date. | Net 45 days. Net 30 or upfront fees require approval. |
| **REQ-07** | Price Escalation Cap | Annual fee increases capped at CPI or a maximum of 3% per year. | 5% flat cap. Variable/unlimited increases prohibited. |
| **REQ-08** | Mutual Indemnification | Vendor provides uncapped indemnity for third-party IP infringement and data breach. | IP indemnity capped at 3x aggregate annual contract value. |
| **REQ-09** | Limitation of Liability Cap | Aggregate liability capped at 1x–2x annual fees; no consequential damage waivers for gross negligence. | Direct damages capped at total fees paid in preceding 12 months. |
| **REQ-10** | Governing Law & Jurisdiction | Governing law must be the State of Delaware or New York with exclusive local venue. | State of Customer headquarters. |
